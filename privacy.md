# KenjaPlayer Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 28, 2026

At KenjaPlayer, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how KenjaPlayer handles your data across all supported platforms (macOS, iOS, and tvOS).

## 1. Zero Data Collection & Sharing
KenjaPlayer is a privacy-first application that processes everything locally on your device.
- **No Personal Data Collection**: We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, identifiers, or usage statistics.
- **Local AI Processing**: All video playback, audio extraction, and AI subtitle generation/translation are processed entirely on your device. None of your media files, audio data, transcribed text, or translation results are ever uploaded to our servers, third-party servers, or any cloud services.
- **No Telemetry**: We do not send any analytics, crash reports, or usage data to external services.

## 2. Media Server Connections
KenjaPlayer allows you to connect to your self-hosted media servers (such as Jellyfin or Plex).
- **Direct Connection Only**: The app communicates directly with servers you configure. We have no intermediary servers and cannot see or access your server credentials or media content.
- **Credentials Stored Locally**: Your server URLs, usernames, and authentication tokens are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to us or any third party.

## 3. AI Models
All AI models used for speech recognition and subtitle generation are bundled within the app. No model downloads or network connections are required for AI features to function.

## 4. Third-Party Services
KenjaPlayer does not integrate any third-party analytics SDKs, user tracking tools, telemetry frameworks, or advertising libraries. We do not track your behavior inside the app.

## 5. Children's Privacy
Since KenjaPlayer does not collect any personal data, we do not knowingly collect, request, or maintain any information from children under the age of 13.
