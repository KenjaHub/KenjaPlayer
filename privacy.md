# KenjaPlayer Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 11, 2026

At KenjaPlayer, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how KenjaPlayer handles your data.

## 1. Zero Data Collection & Sharing
KenjaPlayer is a fully offline, local macOS application.
- **No Personal Data Collection**: We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, identifiers, or usage statistics.
- **Local AI Processing**: All video playback, audio extraction, and AI subtitle generation/translation are processed entirely locally on your Mac. None of your media files, audio data, transcribing text, or translation queries are ever uploaded to our servers, third-party servers, or any cloud services.
- **No External Communication**: The app has no server-side component for handling media. Your content remains completely private on your local machine.

## 2. Model Downloads & Network Activity
When you download or update open-source AI models within the app, the download requests are made directly from your machine to the hosting platform (such as Hugging Face or GitHub). During this process, no user data, media files, or telemetry are transmitted or uploaded.

## 3. Third-Party Services
KenjaPlayer does not integrate any third-party analytics SDKs, user tracking tools, telemetry frameworks, or advertising libraries. We do not track your behavior inside the app.

## 4. Children’s Privacy
Since KenjaPlayer does not collect any personal data or transmit information over the internet, we do not knowingly collect, request, or maintain any information from children under the age of 13.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
