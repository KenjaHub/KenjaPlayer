Two engines, one player

KenjaPlayer routes every file to the engine that plays it best: Apple's AVFoundation when it fits, and a bundled libmpv when it doesn't. You never pick — the right decoder just happens.

  • AVFoundation — native, battery-friendly playback for Apple-native media.
  • libmpv (MPV) — the engine the video community trusts, for everything exotic.
  • Hardware acceleration — 4K and 8K, HDR and 10-bit, decoded on the GPU.
  • Automatic routing — files open instantly in whichever engine fits.

Formats, questions

Can it play RMVB files on Mac and iPhone?
Yes — RMVB and RM are routed through the bundled libmpv engine, alongside AVI, WMV, FLV and the rest of the classic zoo. No converting required.
Does 8K playback really work on iPhone?
8K files decode via hardware acceleration where the chip supports it, and play smoothly on recent Pro devices. Free playback includes a generous 4K/8K allowance; unlimited high-res playback is part of the one-time PRO upgrade.
Does it open files from the Files app / Finder?
Yes — use the Share sheet, Open With, or drag and drop (Mac). Local files, downloads and cloud-provider files in the Files app all open directly.
