# KenjaPlayer for Android Privacy Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

KofukuAI provides KenjaPlayer for Android. This policy describes what the app accesses and what it sends to other services.

## Videos, files, and subtitles

KenjaPlayer opens videos, folders, and subtitle files that you select. The app stores imported files, library information, playback positions, playback history, and settings on your device. You can remove items or clear playback history in the app. Removing a mounted folder revokes app access to the folder. Clearing app storage or uninstalling KenjaPlayer removes its private app data. Files stored outside the app by you or another provider are managed separately.

When you enable AI subtitles, KenjaPlayer sends decoded audio from the selected video to Android's on-device speech recognition service. Android requires microphone permission to use that service. KenjaPlayer supplies the video audio and does not record from the microphone itself. You can revoke permission in Android Settings.

Google ML Kit translates recognized text on your device. KenjaPlayer does not upload your media, subtitle text, or translations for AI processing. Android and ML Kit may connect to download or update supported language models. Google ML Kit and its Firebase components can send diagnostics to Google, including app and device information, installation identifiers, performance and error data, and selected translation languages. For more information, see [Google ML Kit Terms and Privacy](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Web browsing

When you browse or play web media, the selected websites, search providers, and media servers receive your requests directly. They may receive your IP address, search terms, cookies, and other information under their own privacy policies. KenjaPlayer saves browser history and bookmarks on your device. Private browsing uses a separate profile and clears that profile's browsing data when you exit private mode. Private browsing does not prevent websites you visit from receiving requests or keeping their own records. If you unlock protected bookmarks with biometrics, Android performs the check and KenjaPlayer does not receive your biometric data.

## Purchases

The store you use processes payments and supplies purchase information used to confirm, restore, and manage access to Pro. KenjaPlayer does not receive payment card details. The app caches confirmed access on your device for offline use and checks the store again when connected. Stores handle their payment and account records under their own privacy policies. Google Play purchases are covered by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Data retention and contact

KenjaPlayer has no KofukuAI user account or server for your app library, browsing history, or subtitles. KenjaPlayer keeps your library and app settings in private app storage until you remove them, clear app storage, or uninstall. Contact the relevant provider about records held by a store, website, or Google. You can contact KofukuAI at [kenjaplayer@kofukuai.com](mailto:kenjaplayer@kofukuai.com) about this policy or how the app handles data.
